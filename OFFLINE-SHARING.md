# CIMCO inspections: phone to office

## The daily routine

1. **Inspect the house.** Choose rooms in any order. Add or rename areas to match the house. **Save and finish later** keeps the visit on the phone.
2. **Review the visit.** Open **Review / PDF**. Check missing details, observations, photos, and signatures. Unfinished visits are labeled as drafts.
3. **Prepare office packet.** Choose **Share packet**, or **Save packet** if sharing is unavailable. The ZIP contains the inspection, photographs, and printable report. Keep the ZIP together.
4. **Send the ZIP.** Use LocalSend when the phone and Windows computer are on the same local Wi-Fi. No internet or inspection-app account is needed. The office accepts the file in LocalSend.
5. **Office: receive it.** Open **CIMCO-Office-Desk.html**, choose **Receive office file**, and select the ZIP. Check the property and date. Enter the receiving staff member's name and save the receipt file.
6. **Return the receipt.** Send the receipt back to the phone. The inspector imports it with **Receive office file** under **Office tools & backups**. Only a receipt matching that exact packet confirms office receipt. Later changes need a new packet.

Saving or sharing does not prove the office received the file. A receipt records the name entered by staff and the packet version; it is not an authenticated electronic signature.

## Set up once

- Download **LocalSend from https://localsend.org/** on Windows and from its official store links on each iPhone and Android phone. Keep the inspection app on the phones already in use.
- Put both devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi. Guest networks may keep devices apart. Allow LocalSend's normal local-network permission when asked. No need to disable the firewall. A router can provide local Wi-Fi even without an internet connection.
- If LocalSend is missing from the Share menu, save the packet first, then select that ZIP from Files or Downloads inside LocalSend.
- Extract this package to a regular Windows folder. Open **CIMCO-Office-Desk.html** in an up-to-date Edge or Chrome browser. Keep its filename, folder, and browser profile the same: local records depend on them. Do not run from inside the ZIP or in private browsing.
- For training, open **CIMCO-Office-Practice.html** and import **examples/SAMPLE-Office-Packet.zip**. Practice records have separate storage. Every sample name and image is fictional.

The Office Desk includes all required scripts, styles, and logo. No hosted office app, backend, account, or automatic synchronization is needed. Local-file storage support depends on the browser. Prove reopening and backup recovery on the office computer before relying on it.

## Other ways to transfer

A USB cable or removable drive can carry the same ZIP. Android commonly offers file transfer after unlocking. On iPhone, use Files with a compatible external drive or the file-sharing method supported by installed apps. A charge cable alone does not guarantee access to all iPhone files from Windows.

Away from the office, send the saved ZIP through the existing email or messaging service. That delivery service needs a connection; the inspection app still needs no server. Large photo packets may exceed attachment limits. A sent message does not confirm that the office imported the packet.

## Office work

- **Property layouts:** Save a layout from a received visit or create one in **Property layouts**. Include the address and unit. Export the library and import the file on each phone. New visits start with blank results; inspectors can still change rooms. Sharing is manual, with no automatic sync.
- **Maintenance:** Urgent findings appear first. Filter repair or cleaning work, assign a person, record progress and notes, and add completion photos. Office progress stays separate from the signed observations. Prepare a new packet to return office updates to a phone or another computer.
- **Photo comparisons:** Move-out reports match move-in photos by room type, room name, and checklist item. Renamed or unmatched areas are not guessed. A move-out packet includes its available move-in comparison evidence.
- **Recently deleted:** Restore inspections, photos, or removed rooms. Permanently deleting a local copy does not remove copies already exported in other packets or backups.
- **Complete backup:** Save after field work and office updates, with another copy in the office's normal protected backup location. Backups include records, photos, layout library, handoff history, and deleted items. Full backups restore into an empty app to avoid overwriting work. Use ordinary packets to add visits to an app that already has records. Complete backups can include internal access notes; store them accordingly.

## Before using real inspections

Try a fictional visit on one actual iPhone and one Android: reopen offline, take photos, sign, save a PDF, send the packet, receive it on Windows, return a receipt, and reopen after closing the browser. Test a complete backup restore in an empty copy before rollout.

Desktop checks cover packet integrity, reports, receipts, recovery, and offline operations in a local browser preview. They do not establish physical-phone camera, native Share, LocalSend delivery, or direct-file browser behavior. The included standalone Field App is a portable browser option, not a verified iPhone installation method. Updating an existing installed phone app must keep its existing address and paths so saved inspections remain available.
